Settings → Accessibility → Your TV: phone control and typing
Where does the text go?

It goes into the open text field, or becomes a task when Tasks is open. In other apps it goes into their field once the accessibility service is on. Without it, Android doesn’t let one app type into another, so the text is copied to the TV’s clipboard.

Update Your TV

The TV fetches our latest version and checks that it is signed with the same key as the installed app. If all is well, press Install: Android’s install screen opens on the TV, and you confirm with the remote. Nothing is installed without your OK.
