## Per site (league pages)
- FotMob: table has columns # / team / PL / +- / GD / PTS / Form (last 5 as green W, grey D, red L squares) / Next (opponent logo only); zones shown by a thin coloured bar at the row's start plus a legend (Champions League Elite, Qualification, League Two, Relegation); no arrows for position movement. Next/live games: on the fixtures page «By date / By round / By team», dated groups, score or kick-off time in the middle, «FT» on the left for finished; the overview has a «Round N» selector with arrows. Round navigation: left/right arrows or a dropdown of rounds.
- Sofascore: table with a small badge per team showing the AFC zone (colour chip with the zone code), All/Home/Away tabs, Last-5 form squares, a legend «Rules and legend» below; matches list on the left side with «By date / By round» and a «Round N» dropdown with arrows; a featured next match card with 1/X/2 odds on top; heavy page with popups (language) and ads.
- ESPN: plain table: club, GP W D L F A GD P, no form, no zones; schedule is a day strip (Tue..Mon) with arrows and a calendar icon, one table per day with match, time, location (stadium) and odds; very light on data.
- BBC Sport (Premier League): clean table with Played/Won/Drawn/Lost/For/Against/GD/Points and Form (last 5 coloured squares + a trailing «next» square); zone lines drawn as thin separators between rows (e.g. after 4th, 5th), no colours legend in view.
- premierleague.com: table with position, club crest, played, W, D, L, GF, GA, GD, points, Form (last 5) and a Next-opponent crest; a coloured bar at the row start marks zones; cookie gate on first load (not accepted by me).
- spl.com.sa (Roshn official): table with a position arrow (up green / down red / dash = movement since last round), club, P W D L GF GA GD PTS, a coloured left bar for zones and a TREND column with an expand button per club; filter dropdowns «Overall» and season «2026/2027».
- Kooora / Yallakora: only home pages captured (ad/consent gates in front: Kooora shows an Admiral gate; Yallakora a notifications bar); the home has a matches strip (yesterday/today/tomorrow tabs) with a horizontal match carousel, and news below. Table/fixtures pages still to do.

## Per site (club pages)
- FotMob (Al Hilal, team id 2529): header with crest and country, tabs Overview / Table / Fixtures / Squad / Player stats / Team stats / Transfers / History / News. Next/last games: the Next match card plus the scrolling Fixtures list; the form row holds the last results.
