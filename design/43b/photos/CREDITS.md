Photos are from the app's own photo packs (`photos/packs.json` of the repository: haramain, ksa-cities, nature, sea, space), re-saved at 1920 px for these mockups.
Sources: Wikimedia Commons (CC BY, BY-SA, CC0, public domain) and NASA/Hubble (public domain). Each pack's `credits.json` (inside its zip) names the author and licence of every photo;
the app shows them on its «مجموعات الصور» screen. Files here: haramain-night, haramain-dome (haramain 7, 11), alula-rock, riyadh-dusk (ksa-cities 8, 1),
blue-ridges, aurora, dunes (nature 7, 1, 10), sea-sunset, coral (sea 6, 3), saturn (space 5).
