# Packages of a community list that owners often want (field reports: a Xiaomi updater, voice, MiLink, a video player, YouTube Music).
# optimize/community.sh leaves a package whose name matches one of these regular expressions ON and lists it in optimize/run/community-ask.txt:
# the agent asks the person (in Arabic, one line) and, on a yes, adds the packages to optimize/lists/mine.txt. One regular expression per line.
# an update service: the TV would stop getting its system or app updates
(^|\.)(updater|ota|fota|systemupdate|swupdate|otaupdate|upgrade)(\.|$)
updater
# a firmware or app update service by any of its usual names (a Xiaomi TV's com.xiaomi.mitv.updateservice was turned off by a community list)
(^|\.)update(service|r)?(\.|$)
# voice and assistants
voice
assistant
# phone-to-TV connect and casting helpers
milink
# a video or media player
videoplayer|mediaplayer|mediaexplorer
