# NEVER disable, on any TV, from any list: the TV's inputs (HDMI, AV, sources, the tuner) and its keyboards. Regular expressions, one per line,
# matched against the whole package name, as core.txt is. A package under the HDMI, AV or tuner inputs, once disabled, leaves a port that
# notices the cable and stays black («No Signal»), and turning the pieces back on one by one does not bring the picture back: a full restore
# and a cold boot do (Clean Your TV issue #321, github.com/Gathin23/cleanyourtv, MIT). Without a keyboard nobody can type a Wi-Fi password.
# optimize/disable.sh refuses a list that names one; optimize/community.sh and the setup on the TV leave them out. disable.sh also asks the
# TV itself (dumpsys package) and leaves on any package that asks for TV_INPUT_HARDWARE or serves a TV input or a keyboard, whatever its name.
.*tvinput.*
.*hdmi.*
.*tuner.*
.*livetv.*
.*([._]|^)(input)?sources?([._]|$).*
.*sourcemanager.*
.*([._]|^)cec([._]|$).*
.*inputmethod.*
.*keyboard.*
com\.google\.android\.tv
com\.mediatek\.(dtv|tvinput|tvcm|mtkdtv).*
