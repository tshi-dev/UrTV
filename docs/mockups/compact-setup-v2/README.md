# One box, one text style (4.4)

The owner, on the compact screens: «ONE BOX for everything», «ONE TEXT STYLE», and read before the focus goes to a button.
Every page of «إعداد تلفزيوني» and the update, install, rescue and add-on screens is the same box (sa.urtv.home.Box): 560 × 600 dp, capped by the 90 %
safe area (on a 1080p TV at density 2 the screen is 540 dp tall, so the box is 560 × 486 dp), always the same size, in the middle. The title is fixed
at its top; what is under it scrolls inside, with a scroll bar that stays and a «⌄» while there is more. One text style: 22 sp title, 17 sp body (rows
and buttons too), 14 sp secondary; the percentage of the setup uses the title style. When the words are longer than the box, the reading area has the
focus first (blue ring): DOWN scrolls them, and only at their end does DOWN move to the button.

The pictures are HTML drawings at the app's sizes with a stand-in font (DejaVu Sans), 1920×1080 shown at 1280×720; not screenshots of the TV.
