# الكلمة المفقودة (the missing word)

A sentence with one word hidden; the team says it.

## In one line
The TV shows a true statement with a blank. Teams guess in turn; OK reveals.

## Decks (settings chip «النوع»)
- **حكمة ومثل:** sayings and wise phrases from the app's own `wisdom.json` (checked in the repo).
- **معلومة:** statements that come from the verified quiz bank (e.g. «عاصمة مصر هي ___», «الرمز الكيميائي للأكسجين ___»), the answer is the data value, so it is a fact, not a guess.
- **آية** (optional, off by default; the owner decides): one word of a verse from the app's checked `verses.json`; the text is never typed by hand.

## Remote-only flow (default)
1. Ready screen: chips for النوع (مزيج / حكمة / معلومة / آية), الفرق (1 to 4; default 2), عدد الأسئلة (10), button «ابدأ».
2. **Turn.** The statement is shown with the blank as a dotted pill sized to the word length. The team has 15 seconds (setting: 10, 15, 30) to say the word aloud. OK reveals the word; two buttons: «صح» (+10) or «خطأ» (0, and with steal on, the next team gets the same blank for +5).
3. **Hint.** A button «تلميح» (once per question) shows the first letter, costing 5 of the 10 points.
4. Rounds alternate teams; after the last question the winner screen shows.

## Phone flow (optional)
Phones can answer by tapping from four choices (the answer plus 3 plausible words of the same kind) or by typing; the TV shows who answered first. The scoring is the same.

## Edge cases
- If more than one word makes sense (a saying with two accepted forms), the item stores `accept` forms and the reveal shows both.
- Statements never hide a number that depends on counting method (verse counts) and never hide a name that could be spelled two ways without storing both.

## Data
`games/missing-word/items.json` generated by `games/tools/build_words.py`: `text` with `___`, `answer` (ar/en), `accept`, `deck`, `difficulty`, `age`, `source`. Facts from the verified bank; sayings from `assets/shared/wisdom.json`; verses from `assets/shared/verses.json`.
