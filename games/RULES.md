# Rules sheets for the card and board games

For the builders. Each rule says how sure we are: **[checked]** agreed by several rule sources we opened, **[variant]** differs between groups (offer as a setting), **[open]** not checked, ask a Saudi player before building. Sources opened: Wikipedia (Baloot), and search summaries of Pagat, Jawaker, Gamerules and Samsung's Baloot listing for Baloot and Jackaroo; the pages themselves were not reachable from this build environment, so every number below is marked by how many summaries agreed.

## بلوت (Baloot)

- Players: 4, two teams of two, partners sit opposite. Play goes counter-clockwise. Deck: 32 cards (7 to Ace, four suits). [checked]
- Match: first team to **152** points wins. [checked]
- Deal: 5 cards each, then one card face up (the bidding card), then after the bid the rest so that each has 8. [checked in outline]
- Bidding: first round, in turn each player says **صن** (no trump), **حكم** (the suit of the face-up card is trump) or **بس** (pass). If everyone passes, a second round lets a player name a different trump suit or صن. [checked in outline; variants: أشكل, the dealer-side options, the exact order: variant]
- The player who buys takes the face-up card, the others are dealt the rest. The team that bought must win more than half of the round's points or it loses them to the opponents. [variant: how a tie and a failed bid are scored]
- Following: you must follow the suit led; in حكم you must trump if you cannot follow and must overtrump when you can. [variant: partner exemption]
- Card points. حكم, trump suit: Jack **20**, Nine **14**, Ace 11, Ten 10, King 4, Queen 3, Eight and Seven 0. Plain suits (and all cards in صن): Ace 11, Ten 10, King 4, Queen 3, Jack 2, Nine, Eight, Seven 0. [checked]
- Round total: **162** in حكم and **130** in صن, which includes 10 for the last trick. [checked]
- Game points per round (ابناط to points): حكم round points divided by 10 (rounded), صن doubled and divided by 10, so a full round is 16 in حكم and 26 in صن. [checked in outline; rounding rule is a variant]
- Projects (مشاريع), declared on the first trick: سرا (3 in a row) 20, خمسين (4 in a row) 50, مئة (5 in a row, or four equal tens, jacks, queens, kings) 100, 400 (four aces, صن only). In game points: 2, 5, 10, and 40 in صن. [checked: values; the exact legal combinations: variant]
- بلوت project: King and Queen of the trump suit, 20, حكم only. [checked]
- Taking all 8 tricks (كبوت): bonus. [open: the number differs a lot between sources; make it a setting]
- Doubling (دبل, ثري, فور, قهوة) is a house rule. [variant, off by default]
- TV design notes: each phone shows its own hand; the TV shows the table, trump, score, and whose turn it is; a lock screen when the remote is passed.

## جاكارو (Jackaroo)

- Players: 4 in two teams (partners opposite); 6 players variant exists. Each player has 4 marbles; the board has a home base, a track, and a safe lane into the final base. [checked]
- Goal: a team wins when all 8 of its marbles reach the final spots; when your own marbles are done you move your partner's. [checked]
- Cards drive moves: a 52-card deck without jokers in the Jawaker rules; some boxed sets add jokers. [variant]
- Ace or King brings a marble out of the starting yard onto the start spot. [checked]
- 7 can be split between two marbles; 4 moves backwards; Jack swaps positions with another marble. [checked in outline]
- A marble that sits on its start spot or in its safe lane cannot be passed, captured or swapped. [checked in outline]
- Number cards move that many steps; 10, Queen, King and Ace values, the effect of 5, and whether a player who cannot play burns a card: [open, rules differ by publisher: pick one named version and write it down with a Saudi player]
- Capturing: landing on an opponent sends it back to its yard. [checked in outline]
- TV design notes: the board is shared and big, the hand is on the phone; undo is not allowed.

## دومينو (Dominoes)

- 28 tiles (double-six). [checked, ar.wikipedia]
- Two players take 7 tiles each and the rest is the boneyard (السحب); with 3 players, 7 each; with 4 players, 7 each and no boneyard. [checked, ar.wikipedia]
- The holder of the highest double begins (variants: highest total). [variant]
- A tile is placed when one of its halves matches an open end; a player with no legal tile draws until one fits or the boneyard is empty, then passes. [checked]
- The first to lay down all tiles wins the round and scores the pips left in the opponents' hands. A blocked game goes to the lowest pip count. [checked in outline]
- Target score (100 or 150 or 200) is a setting. [variant]
- TV design notes: the chain on the TV, hands on phones, or on the TV with a "hide" screen when passing the remote.
