## What to look for, riskiest first
1. **خمّن الكلمة: الإجابات and كلمات مقبولة.** The answers (511) are common words picked by a dictionary rule, minus a blocklist; an unsuitable or obscure word can slip through. The accepted guesses (about 31,000) are every word form the dictionary has, so odd forms are expected there; only a word that is vulgar or sectarian matters.
2. **الكلمة المفقودة: الأقوال** (117 sayings: 57 from the app's own checked list, 60 written from memory). Check each is a real, well-known saying with correct wording and no insult to a group.
3. **خانات** (about 840 sample answers over nine boxes): wrong box (a bird under plants), a name that is not common.
4. **واحد مو معنا and بدون كلام / وصّف** (692 and 714 words): anything unsuitable to act out (the lists leave out sacred acts and places on purpose), a word that is dialect-only, a wrong English gloss.
5. **أسئلة وجواب**: facts are checked against sources (see VERIFY.md), so look at the Arabic wording and the wrong options (a wrong option that is also right).

## How to mark
Copy the word or the line and write one of: «احذف» (remove), «صحّح: ...» (fix), «لهجة» (dialect only). Send the list back; nothing else is needed.
